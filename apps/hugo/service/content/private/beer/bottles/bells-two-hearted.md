# Why I Bought It

Bell's Two Hearted is one of those beers that has earned its reputation for a reason.

It's a very good IPA.

For my palate, though, I find myself comparing it to beers like Lagunitas Maximus, and that's where the differences become more obvious.

Two Hearted has a nice malt presence, but it is lighter and less substantial than Maximus.

It is still creamy, still balanced, and still a very enjoyable IPA.

But when choosing between the two, I'm reaching for Maximus.

# Appearance

Amber to deep golden.

The beer has a richer color than many modern pale IPAs and presents itself as a traditional American IPA.

The appearance immediately suggests that there is some malt character underneath the hops.

---

# Aroma

Fresh hop character with a noticeable malt presence underneath.

The aroma is clean and inviting without being overly aggressive.

It has the classic American IPA character I expect from a beer with this kind of reputation.

---

# Flavor

Two Hearted is a balanced IPA.

The hops are present, but the beer isn't simply a bitterness bomb.

There is a noticeable malt backbone that gives the beer some substance.

Compared with Maximus, however, I find Two Hearted to be lighter.

Maximus brings more malt depth and more overall body.

Two Hearted feels like a cleaner, lighter expression of the same general idea:

Hops with enough malt to keep the beer balanced.

It's very good.

But for my palate, I want just a little more weight behind the hops.

---

# Mouthfeel

Creamy and smooth.

There is enough body to keep the beer from feeling thin, but it is still noticeably lighter than Maximus.

This is probably the biggest difference between the two beers for me.

Two Hearted is creamy.

Maximus is **creamier and more substantial**.

That extra body is what ultimately pushes Maximus ahead for me.

---

# Finish

The finish is clean with lingering hop character and a light bitterness.

It doesn't become overly aggressive or harsh.

The lighter body makes it easy to drink, and the finish encourages another sip without overwhelming the palate.

---

# What I Liked

- Very well-balanced IPA.
- Creamy mouthfeel.
- Noticeable malt backbone.
- Amber appearance.
- Clean and approachable hop character.
- Easy to drink.
- Classic American IPA profile.
- Enough body to avoid feeling thin.

Most importantly, Two Hearted is a beer that understands balance.

It doesn't need to go overboard with bitterness or hop intensity.

---

# What I'd Change

For my personal palate, I would like more body.

The malt character is there, but I want more of it.

Compared with Maximus, Two Hearted feels lighter and less substantial.

That isn't necessarily a flaw in the beer.

It's simply where my preferences have landed.

I like my IPAs to have enough malt and body to stand up against the hops.

Maximus does that better for me.

---

# Comparison Notes

This is where the difference becomes clear.

Both beers have:

- Malt presence
- Hop character
- Creamy mouthfeel
- Good balance
- Strong drinkability

But Maximus has more of what I personally value.

# Final Thoughts

Bell's Two Hearted is a very good beer.

I understand why it has become such a respected American IPA.

For me, though, the comparison to Maximus is unavoidable.

Two Hearted is:

- Amber
- Creamy
- Malty
- Balanced
- Easy to drink

But it is also lighter.

Maximus brings more body and a stronger malt foundation, which is exactly what I have come to appreciate more as my palate has evolved.

I've realized that I don't necessarily want an IPA that is just about hops.

I want the malt to matter too.

I want body.

I want texture.

I want the beer to feel substantial.

Two Hearted gets me part of the way there.

Maximus gets me further.

So while I would happily drink Two Hearted any day, if I have to choose between the two, I'm taking Maximus **7 times out of 10**.

Two Hearted is a very good IPA.

Maximus is more my IPA.
