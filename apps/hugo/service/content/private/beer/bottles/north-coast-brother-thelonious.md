# Why I Bought It

Brother Thelonious is one of the classic American interpretations of a Belgian Dark Strong Ale.

Unlike many modern craft beers chasing extreme hops or pastry-style sweetness, this beer focuses on rich Belgian yeast character, dark fruit, caramelized malt, and restrained bitterness.

As someone who generally prefers Belgian ales and Imperial Stouts over aggressively hopped beers, this style naturally caught my attention.
