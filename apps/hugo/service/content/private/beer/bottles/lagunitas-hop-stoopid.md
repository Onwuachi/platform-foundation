# Why I Bought It

I picked up Hop Stoopid because I wanted to start a deeper dive into one of my favorite breweries: **Lagunitas Brewing Company**.

At **$9.99 for a 6-pack**, this was an excellent value for an 8.0% Imperial IPA. It also gave me a chance to revisit a beer that sits in the same general hop-heavy world as one of my longtime personal favorites, **Lagunitas Maximus**.

This is a very good beer. But Maximus is still the benchmark I compare Lagunitas hop-forward beers against.

# Appearance

Hop Stoopid pours a darker golden color that leans toward amber.

It has more depth of color than a typical bright golden IPA, giving it a richer visual appearance that fits the beer's heavier hop profile.

---

# Aroma

The nose brings:

* Citrus
* Sweet hops
* A noticeable hop-forward character

The aroma is inviting without coming across as overwhelmingly aggressive.

---

# Flavor

The first impression is distinctly hoppy, with **pineapple** coming forward early.

The 102 IBU bitterness is present, but the beer does not immediately come across as harsh or punishing. Instead, the hop character and fruitiness work together before the bitterness settles in.

There is a **light lingering bitterness** that remains after the initial sip.

The interesting part is that the beer is juicy enough to make you want to take another sip and explore what comes next.

It invites inquiry.

---

# Mouthfeel

Hop Stoopid has enough body to support its 8.0% ABV and heavy hop load without becoming overly thick or heavy.

The overall experience is balanced between juicy hop character and lingering bitterness.

---

# Finish

The finish is defined by a **light, lingering bitterness**.

It stays with you without completely overwhelming the palate, encouraging another sip and allowing the citrus, tropical fruit, and hop character to continue developing.

---

# What I Liked

* Excellent value at **$9.99 for a 6-pack**.
* Strong 8.0% ABV Imperial IPA.
* 102 IBU gives it serious hop credibility.
* Pineapple and citrus provide a juicy counterpoint to the bitterness.
* Sweet hop character on the nose.
* The bitterness lingers but does not completely dominate.
* Very drinkable for the intensity of the beer.
* It makes you want to keep tasting and exploring the hop profile.

---

# What I'd Change

The biggest issue is not really with Hop Stoopid itself.

It's that I have a **very high personal benchmark for Lagunitas hop-forward beer**.

For me, **Maximus** has been the go-to Lagunitas beer and a personal favorite for years. Hop Stoopid is excellent, but it does not replace Maximus as my benchmark.

---

# Comparison Notes

## Hop Stoopid vs. Maximus

Hop Stoopid:

* 8.0% ABV
* 102 IBU
* More amber/dark golden in appearance
* Citrus and sweet hop nose
* Pineapple-forward flavor
* Juicy and inviting
* Light lingering bitterness

Maximus:

* 9.0% ABV
* 60 IBU
* Longtime personal go-to
* A beer I have returned to for years
* My personal Lagunitas benchmark

The comparison is interesting because the beers approach hop intensity differently.

Hop Stoopid brings a higher stated IBU and a more overt bitter-hop profile, while Maximus delivers a larger overall impression of boldness, body, and hop character.

**Hop Stoopid is a very good Imperial IPA. Maximus is still my Lagunitas standard.**

# Final Thoughts

Hop Stoopid is exactly the kind of beer that makes a Lagunitas deep dive worthwhile.

It is hop-forward without being one-dimensional. The beer starts with citrus and sweet hops, moves into pineapple and juicy fruit character, and then settles into a light lingering bitterness.

The result is a beer that is **very drinkable while still reminding you that you are drinking an 8.0% Imperial IPA with 102 IBU**.

At $9.99 for a 6-pack, the value is excellent.

But there is an important distinction in my personal ranking:

**Hop Stoopid is a very good Lagunitas beer. Maximus is the one I have been drinking for years and the beer I still consider my personal go-to.**

This review is the beginning of a deeper look at Lagunitas — one of my favorite breweries — and the beers that earned their place in my personal beer history.

Excellent value at $9.99 for a 6-pack.

Not better than my longtime Lagunitas favorite, **Maximus**, but absolutely worthy of a place in the Lagunitas rotation.
