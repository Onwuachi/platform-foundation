# Why I Bought It

After enjoying Bell's original Hopslam and rating it as one of my favorite Double IPAs, I wanted to see what the Citrus Hopslam variation actually changed.

The goal was not simply to find a beer with more citrus. I wanted to compare it directly against the original and see whether the citrus worked with the honey character that makes Hopslam distinctive.

I picked up a 4-pack of 16 oz cans for $12.99.

# Aroma

The Citrus Hopslam immediately shows more citrus character than the original Hopslam.

The citrus is more noticeable and more forward, giving the beer a brighter profile while still retaining the underlying honey character associated with Hopslam.

---

# Flavor

Compared directly with the original Hopslam, the difference is clear.

There is **more citrus**, but the more interesting change is how that citrus interacts with the honey sweetness.

The citrus does not simply overpower the honey. Instead, the two seem more integrated, creating a brighter version of the original while retaining the characteristic honey sweetness.

The result is a variation that still tastes recognizably like Hopslam rather than simply becoming another citrus-heavy DIPA.

# Where It Fits

Citrus Hopslam fits alongside the original Hopslam rather than replacing it.

The original remains the benchmark for the classic honey-and-citrus Hopslam profile, while Citrus Hopslam shifts the balance toward brighter citrus without abandoning the honey sweetness.

That makes it particularly interesting as a comparison beer.

---

# Final Thoughts

The first impression is that Bell's got the important part right.

Citrus Hopslam is not simply Hopslam with more citrus thrown at it.

The noticeable difference is the **integration**.

Compared with the original, I get more citrus and a stronger sense of the citrus working together with the honey sweetness. The honey does not disappear, and the beer still retains the character that made the original Hopslam so impressive.

That makes this a worthwhile variation rather than a gimmick.

The real test will be continued side-by-side tasting with the original Hopslam.
