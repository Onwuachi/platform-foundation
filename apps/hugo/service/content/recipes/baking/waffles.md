# Overview

Homemade buttermilk waffles, dialed in over 7+ years of iteration. Uses a quick homemade buttermilk (acid + milk) rather than store-bought, and batches well for the freezer.

<!--more-->

## Ingredients

### Homemade Buttermilk

- 1/2 lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp distilled vinegar
- 1 3/4 cup milk (2% lactose-free, or whole milk)

### Batter

- 2 cups unenriched organic flour
- 1 tbsp baking powder
- 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
- 1 tbsp organic sugar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 2 pasture-raised eggs
- 1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, melted
- 1/4 cup avocado oil
- 1 tbsp cinnamon
- 1 tbsp vanilla extract (homemade optional)

## Equipment

- Waffle iron
- Mixing bowls
- Parchment paper
- Ziplock freezer bags

## Instructions

### 1. Make the buttermilk

Combine lemon juice, distilled vinegar, and milk. Let sit at least 15–30 minutes until curdled/thickened.

### 2. Mix the batter

Combine flour, baking powder, organic sugar, brown sugar, and salt. Add eggs, melted butter, avocado oil, cinnamon, vanilla, and the prepared buttermilk. Mix until just combined.

### 3. Cook

Waffle iron on mid setting (~3). Cook until golden and crisp.

### 4. Freeze for later

Separate cooked waffles with parchment paper and store in a ziplock bag in the freezer.
