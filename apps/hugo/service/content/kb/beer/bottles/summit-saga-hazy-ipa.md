# Why I Bought It

I picked this one up because it's a Minnesota beer from Summit Brewing Co. and the price was hard to ignore — $7.99 for a 4-pack of pint cans.

It's also a good example of a Hazy IPA that doesn't go as heavy on the haze or hop intensity as many New England-style IPAs.

For me, this is more of an easy-drinking everyday IPA than a special shelf bottle.

Summit describes Sága Hazy IPA as having tropical fruit character with notes of mango, grapefruit, tangerine, and passion fruit, along with sourdough and bread crust. The brewery describes the beer as having a pillowy head, soft bitterness, and a smooth, flavorful finish.

# Appearance

**Cloudy with a chance of... lol.**

The beer pours a cloudy, yellowish-gold color, but it isn't nearly as hazy as some of the more aggressively hazy New England-style IPAs.

It has the expected cloudy appearance, but the overall presentation is cleaner and lighter than many modern Hazy IPAs.

# Aroma

Fresh malt, light floral notes, and a clean hop presence.

The aroma isn't overwhelmingly tropical or aggressively hop-forward. Instead, it comes across as relatively fresh and approachable, with the malt character helping balance the hops.

# Flavor

Hoppy, but not as aggressively hoppy as the original Sága.

The hop character is present without dominating the beer. Compared with heavier New England IPAs, this one leans lighter and cleaner.

I'm getting more of a **light caramel and toast** character underneath the hops rather than a huge tropical-fruit explosion.

It's flavorful without becoming a hop bomb.

# Mouthfeel

Fresh, clean, and relatively light.

There is almost a light citrus-zest quality to the mouthfeel that keeps the beer refreshing.

Despite being a Hazy IPA, it doesn't have the heavy, almost juice-like body that some modern hazies have.

# Finish

Standard creamy finish that isn't overpowering.

The beer finishes cleanly and smoothly, making it very easy to drink.

At 7.0% ABV, it drinks more like a **session-style beer** than I would expect from the ABV on paper.

That makes it approachable, but it also means it doesn't have the weight or intensity I personally look for when I'm reaching for a more substantial IPA.

# What I Liked

* It's a Minnesota beer from Summit Brewing Co.
* Good value at $7.99 for a 4-pack of pint cans.
* Clean and fresh drinking experience.
* Hoppy without becoming overwhelming.
* Lighter and more approachable than many New England-style IPAs.
* Light caramel and toast notes add some malt character.
* Creamy, smooth finish.
* Easy to drink.
* Works well as an everyday beer.

# What I'd Change

I'd like to see a little more ABV and intensity.

For my personal taste, the beer leans slightly too far toward the lighter side. At 7.0% ABV, it drinks very easily, but I wouldn't mind seeing a little more body, hop depth, or overall intensity.

The beer is good at what it does, but I'm usually looking for something with a little more weight and character.

# Final Thoughts

Summit Sága Hazy IPA is a very approachable Hazy IPA that doesn't go overboard with haze, hop intensity, or bitterness.

For me, the biggest strength is its drinkability. It has enough hop character to remain interesting, but the lighter body and clean finish make it feel closer to a session beer than a heavy modern New England IPA.

The $7.99 price for a 4-pack of pint cans makes the value even better.

Would I choose this over a bigger, more intense IPA? Probably not.

But that's not really the point of this beer.

This is a **good Minnesota everyday pour** — fresh, clean, hoppy enough, easy to drink, and inexpensive.

At 8.0, it's a strong beer for the money and a good example of a Hazy IPA that doesn't require you to commit to a giant hop bomb.
