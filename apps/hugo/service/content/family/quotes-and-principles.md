# Quotes & Principles

A collection of quotes, principles, observations, and mental models worth keeping.

---

## Platform Engineering

> **Enterprise-grade platform engineering focused on reliability, automation, and operational excellence.**

> **Stop owning fires 🔥 ❌ — start owning modules 🚀 ✅**

> **I am not stuck. I am just in between phases.**

> *"You merely adopted the darkness; I was born in it."* — Bane 🦇

> *Given the world as it actually exists, where are the leverage points available to me?*

## Work & Freedom

> **work hard → become valuable → succeed → become free.**

The promise we were given.

> **work hard + acquire scarce skills + know people + catch opportunities + manage capital + avoid catastrophic setbacks + be lucky + keep doing it for decades.**

The actual game.

> **The point of building wealth is not merely to accumulate money. It is to increase the number of choices available to you.**

> **Don't spend your entire life becoming valuable to a system that never becomes valuable to you.**

> **You don't have to play every part of it.**

> **Play enough of it to protect yourself and the people you love.**

> **Then spend the rest of your life building something that actually feels like yours.**

---

## Labor & Life

> **How much of a person's life should have to be sold to receive access to housing, food, healthcare, security, education and leisure?**

> **A man can become extremely good at keeping a life running that he doesn't actually enjoy living.**

> **Sometimes “fuck it” isn't “I don't care anymore.” Sometimes it's “I am exhausted from caring about something that doesn't seem to care back.”**

---

## Love, Desire & Relationships

> **Being valuable is not the same thing as being desired.**

> **You cannot work your way into being desired. You cannot provide your way into admiration. You cannot sacrifice your way into respect.**

> **Need is not desire. Provision is not intimacy. Staying is not choosing. Functioning is not flourishing.**

> **You cannot force love, attraction, admiration, or care.**

> **A person can be extraordinarily useful and still not be wanted.**

> **Her body belongs to her. Your life belongs to you.**

> **Don't enter an arrangement whose terms you fundamentally don't want, then spend twenty years trying to convince yourself the arrangement is normal.**

> **Don't keep investing attention, money, sex, resources, or emotional energy into someone whose behavior doesn't align with what you actually want.**

> **Don't confuse generosity with purchasing commitment.**

> **The goal isn't to control what another person gives you. The goal is to decide what kind of relationship you are willing to live in.**

> **You don't have to force someone to choose you. You do have to choose what you will accept.**

---

## Autonomy & Letting Go

> **Don't try to cage someone who doesn't want to be with you. Don't chase. Don't negotiate against yourself. Let people reveal what they actually want, and then act accordingly.**

> **Don't become the mule trying to build a cage somebody is already trying to escape.**

> **Treat a bird like a bird. If a bird wants to fly, let her fly high.**

> **Let people choose. Then believe their choice.**

> **If someone wants to leave, don't build a cage. Open the door.**

> **Your job is not to convince someone to stay. Your job is to decide whether you want someone who has to be convinced.**

> **Never sacrifice the foundation of your life to preserve a relationship that no longer preserves you.**

---

## Reciprocity & Self-Respect

> **Don't keep carrying more in the hope that eventually your value will be recognized.**

> **Trying to purchase reciprocity after the evidence has already shown that reciprocity isn't naturally occurring is not love; it is self-abandonment.**

> **Being needed is not the same as being loved. Being useful is not the same as being valued.**

> **Don't confuse fulfilling your role with having a relationship.**

> **A man can conquer the world and still lose himself trying to keep one person.**

> **Do not betray your principles to keep someone who would not betray theirs to keep you.**

> **Don't throw away the result of twenty years of discipline for twenty minutes of emotion.**

> **A man's greatest vulnerability is not that he lacks power. It is that he may surrender his power when he becomes emotionally attached.**

> **Build your life on principles strong enough to survive love, lust, fear, and loss.**

> **Don't backstab your own future to protect your present attachment.**

> **You can lose money and rebuild it. You can lose status and rebuild it. But betraying your own principles teaches you that they were never really principles.**

> **Never make an irreversible decision to solve a temporary emotional problem.**

> **A relationship should fit into the life you built—not require you to destroy the life you built to keep it.**

---

## Capital, Class & Generational Starting Points

> **I don't want the fact that I started behind to be used as proof that I deserve to stay behind.**

> **Why did I have to climb this whole fucking mountain just to reach a place somebody else could inherit?**

> **Bootstraps can change an individual's position. They do not automatically erase a starting-position differential compounded across generations.**

> **Hard work can change your position without proving that the game started fair.**

> **Ownership compounds. Home equity compounds. Retirement accounts compound. Business ownership compounds. Inheritance compounds.**

> **You can succeed individually while unequal starting positions continue to reproduce themselves across generations.**

> **The question isn't whether I can climb. The question is why some people were allowed to start halfway up the mountain and then told the rest of us the mountain was fair.**

---

## Core Principles

> **Do not become so attached to another person that you become willing to betray yourself.**

> **Don't sacrifice your future to preserve your present attachment.**

> **Protect your ability to choose.**

> **Build enough wealth, skill, knowledge, and independence that you are never completely trapped by one job, one institution, one relationship, or one bad decision.**

> **Value yourself enough to walk away from what repeatedly destroys your peace, dignity, or principles.**

> **Build yourself. Build ownership. Protect your freedom. Don't trade your principles for attachment. Don't confuse being useful with being loved. And don't keep carrying something simply because you've al ready carried it for a long time.**
