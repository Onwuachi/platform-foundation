# 2026 Minnesota State Fair

**Date:** September 7, 2026  
**Occasion:** Labor Day / Kids Day  
**Who:** Dad + the boys

## Overview

The plan was to take the boys to the Minnesota State Fair early on Labor Day, spend a good part of the morning there, and head home before everyone was completely exhausted.

That did not exactly happen.

We ended up staying until close to **4:00 PM**.

And somehow the boys' favorite part of the entire State Fair was the **arcade**.

We literally went to the State Fair and went to the arcade **twice**.

---

## The Unexpected Start

We left home at approximately **7:42 AM**.

I even had enough time to stop and get gas on the way.

That turned out to be an interesting little detail because shortly after arriving, some guy gave us **three State Fair tickets**.

Just like that.

Three tickets.

That immediately saved us a decent amount of money and was an unexpected way to start the day.

I have to admit that I've been pretty disappointed in people as of late.

So when an act of kindness like that happens — especially from a complete stranger — I'm always shocked.

It makes me stop and rethink or recalibrate how and what I think about people.

One person's kindness doesn't erase the bad experiences that can happen in life, but it is a good reminder that there are still people who will simply do something good for someone else without expecting anything in return.

I'm thankful and grateful for the gesture.

And honestly, that may end up being one of the things I remember most about this particular Fair trip.

---

## Food First

We started with Fair food.

### First stop

- Footlong hot dog
- Sweet Martha's Cookies
- Milk

A pretty solid way to start the morning.

---

## The Big Hit — The Arcade

Then came the unexpected favorite.

### The arcade.

The boys absolutely loved it.

So much that we went **twice**.

It was one of those funny parenting moments where you realize:

> We came all the way to the Minnesota State Fair and the thing they really wanted to do was play arcade games.

Kids.

But honestly, they were having fun, and that's what mattered.

---

## More Food

After the first arcade visit we grabbed:

- Fries
- Lemonade

Then we headed toward the animal areas.

---

# The Animals

We went through the animal barns and saw:

- Dairy pigs
- Piglets
- Cows
- Baby cows

The boys enjoyed seeing the animals and especially the babies.

We also washed our hands afterward.

Even though we didn't actually touch anything.

Still counts.

---

# Coliseum — Horse Racing

Next we went to the Coliseum and watched the horses doing the quick-turn races.

It was a completely different experience from the arcade and animal barns and gave the boys something else to watch.

---

# Snow Cone, Ice Cream & Rides

After that came:

- Snow cone
- Ice cream
- Rides

We found some rides the boys wanted to go on and let them have their fun.

Eventually we also found one of the cooling fans, which was definitely appreciated.

---

# Minnesota DNR Building

One of the biggest surprises of the day was the **Minnesota DNR building**.

We spent **more than an hour** there.

The boys were genuinely interested and had a great time exploring everything.

We even saw **Smokey the Bear**.

This ended up being one of the longest stops of the day and one of the places where the boys seemed genuinely engaged rather than simply moving from attraction to attraction.

---

# Minnesota Fish Pond

We also saw the live fish pond showing Minnesota fish and wildlife.

That was another good stop and fit naturally with the DNR visit.

---

# Back to the Arcade

And naturally...

We went back to the arcade.

Because apparently that was the real reason we came to the State Fair.

---

# Watching the Advanced Rides

We also watched people going on some of the more advanced rides.

The boys were interested.

I told them:

> We'll save this for another time.

And honestly...

**Not me.**

I'm perfectly happy watching someone else get launched, spun, dropped, or otherwise question their life choices.

---

# Getting Home

We stayed at the Fair until close to **4:00 PM**.

My phone eventually died.

Fortunately, I was still able to get us home.

The day ended up being much longer than originally planned, but the boys had a great time.

---

# What the Boys Loved

The clear winners:

1. **Arcade**
2. Rides
3. Animals and baby animals
4. Minnesota DNR building
5. Ice cream / snow cone
6. Fair food

The arcade winning twice is still hilarious.

---

# What Worked

- Leaving around 7:42 AM
- Driving instead of taking the bus
- Getting gas before the Fair
- Having flexibility in the schedule
- Letting the boys choose some activities
- Not trying to see every part of the Fair
- Taking breaks when needed
- Finding cooling fans
- Spending real time at the DNR building
- Allowing the day to develop naturally instead of forcing the original schedule

---

# What Didn't

### Phone battery

The phone dying was completely avoidable.

That became more important than expected because the Fair day lasted much longer than planned.

---

# What I'd Do Differently

Next time:

- [ ] Fully charge phone before leaving
- [ ] Bring portable battery
- [ ] Bring charging cable
- [ ] Save parking location before entering
- [ ] Take a screenshot of important directions/maps
- [ ] Don't assume the outing will only last a few hours

The original plan was to leave around 1–2 PM.

Instead, we were there until approximately 4 PM.

That's fine when everyone is still having a good time, but it changes the preparation requirements.

---

# Best Part of the Day

The best part may actually have happened before we even got into the Fair.

Someone gave us three tickets.

That simple gesture saved us money and reminded me that people can still surprise you in a good way.

I've been pretty disappointed in people lately.

Today was a good reminder not to let the bad experiences completely overwrite the good ones.

Sometimes someone does something kind for no particular reason.

When that happens, it's worth noticing.

---

# Family Takeaway

The State Fair was crowded, huge, expensive, and exhausting.

But it was also a really good day with the boys.

They got to:

- Eat Fair food
- Play games
- Ride rides
- See animals
- See baby animals
- Watch horse racing
- Learn about Minnesota wildlife
- See Smokey the Bear
- Eat too much sugar
- Explore
- Spend time together

And apparently the most important thing was playing arcade games twice.

That's okay.

Those are the kinds of days that become family memories.

---

# Would We Do It Again?

**Yes.**

But next time:

- Go early
- Keep the schedule flexible
- Bring a portable charger
- Don't try to do everything
- Let the boys pick some things
- Expect the arcade to somehow become a major attraction

And remember that sometimes the best part of the day is something you never planned for.
